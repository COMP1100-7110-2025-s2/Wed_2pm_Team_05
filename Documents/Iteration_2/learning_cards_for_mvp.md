# Learning Cards for MVP

## Learning Card 1:  Students will be more on track if they are offered backup options for their degrees.  

### **HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that offering backup options (e.g., if a course is full, suggesting an equivalent or summer course) will keep students on track.

### **OBSERVATION**
We observed that most students will find a summer course very useful as it would help save them a lot of stress, time and uncertainty, especially in a situation when the course they wanted to take is no longer available. It would be especially great guidance for first year students. However, there are some students who have shown that they would prefer to trust their own plan.

### **CONCLUSION**
From that we learnt that most students will gain a lot of help if they have access to further automatic alternatives especially a summer course instead of having to plan one manually.

### **ACTION**
Therefore, we will input a feature in our website where we will able to process custom-planned automatics alternatives for students using our website especially those going through an unavailable one.


## Learning Card 2: Displaying a visual timeline of degrees side by side would be much clearer to students compared to a simple course list. 

### **HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that showing students a visual timeline of both degrees side by side will reduce confusion compared to just a list of courses.

### **OBSERVATION**
We observed that most students will find it a lot easier with a visual timeline of their degrees, especially those who are visual learners. However, there are students who aren’t visual learners who have input that it won’t matter to them regardless.

### **CONCLUSION**
From that we learnt that most students are visual learners, and the visual timeline will help reduce confusion and frustration as it would give them an easier and peaceful way to plan their weekly journey.

### **ACTION**
Therefore, we will place importance in including a visual timetable in our course selection website instead of just a simple course list.


## Learning Card 3: Dual-degree students will be much less overwhelmed if they have access to case studies from past students with the same dual degrees.

### **HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that short case studies from past dual-degree students (e.g., “How I managed my workload” or “Why I chose summer courses”) will help new students feel less overwhelmed.

### **OBSERVATION**
We observed that most students don’t have much or any experience meeting someone who has given talks about their degree and they would take the opportunity to hear about the studies of past students/people who have previously taken the course. Having access to short case studies immediately will also save a lot of time and stress for students, especially for those who have made the effort to speak with their seniors, but it becomes to no avail. However, some dual degree students thinks that listening to how other people go through their course won’t change the way she will go through about it, so that might reflect what a minority of students will think.

### **CONCLUSION**
From that we learnt most students would be able to less overwhelmed and stressed about their school lifestyle if they saw short case studies from past dual degree students, especially those who need advice. However, some have also input that even if they saw it, its effect on how they do their courses is indefinite. 

### **ACTION**
Therefore, we will try to introduce a new feature where students can access past short case studies from graduating students. However, we would place less priority on this given it might be a less practical feature, alongside the time and effort to make it.


## Learning Card 4: Students will be able to less rely on trial-and-error planning without fear if they have a space to ask questions to past or current students.

### **HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that providing a space to ask questions to past or current students (like a moderated forum or chat) will reduce reliance on trial-and-error planning.

### **OBSERVATION**
We observed all students have input it would indeed be helpful if they have a space to discuss their challenges with past students who could give them practical advice and tips.

### **CONCLUSION**
From that we learnt that students would appreciate it if they have a website where they could discuss and ask for advice from seniors or past students studying the same degree, thus giving them a clearer understanding of how to go about their degree.

### **ACTION**
Therefore, we will introduce go forward with the discussion page or chat feature, where students can ask questions about courses and past students can give their own feedback.
