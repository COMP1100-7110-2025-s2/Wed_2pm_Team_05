## Hypothesis 1: Students doing Dual Degrees will be more Confident they are assigned a Mentor. 

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that matching students with a mentor who did the same dual-degree combo will increase confidence more than generic advice.

**TEST**
To verify that, we will conduct interviews with people doing dual degrees, asking their input on being matched with a mentor with the same dual-degree combo.

**METRIC**
And measure their level of confidence/ comfort if they have an assigned mentor.

**CRITERIA**
We are right if people doing dual degrees report that they will feel much more confident if they are matched with a mentor with the same dual degree combo.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
*ask if they have dual degrees first* 
1. Do you think you would gain confidence if you were assigned a mentor with the same dual degree combo in the next semester?
2. If so, how confident would you be from a scale of 1 to 10? 
3. Do you think it would be helpful to your studies?

## Hypothesis 3: Students will be More on Track if Automatic Alternatives were offered on the event they encounter a full course. 

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that offering automatic alternatives when students encounter a course is full (summer option, swap, or path change) will keep them on track.

**TEST**
To verify that, we will interview people on their input on being offered alternatives if they counter a full course. 

**METRIC**
And measure how much they think such alternatives will keep them on track.

**CRITERIA**
We are right if most students say automatic alternatives will help them stay on track with their studies.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1. In the event you encounter a full course, do you have personal alternatives?
2. If you had the access to automatic alternatives (eg summer course, path change) when encountering such event, would you use it?
3. If so, on a scale from 1 to 10, how helpful do you think it will be in keeping students like you on track?
