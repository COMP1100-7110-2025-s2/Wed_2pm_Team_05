# Interview Questions for Iteration 2

1. Who do you usually turn to for advice about course selection, and why?

2. Would your confidence in course selection change if the advice came from someone with the same dual-degree background? Why or why not?

3. If so, how confident would you be from a scale of 1 to 10? 

4. Do you think it would be helpful to your studies?

5. Do you think tailoring course options to your preferences (like avoiding finals or group work) would help you make course selections? 

6. If so, how much easier do you think it would be (from a scale of 1 to 10)? 

7. Tell me about a time when a course you wanted was full. How did it affect you ?

8. How do you usually figure out alternatives when enrollment doesn’t go as planned?

9. If you had the access to automatic alternatives (eg summer course, path change) when encountering such event, would you use it?

10. If so, on a scale from 1 to 10, how helpful do you think it will be in keeping students like you on track?

11. Have you ever considered changing your major or minor? If so, what kinds of questions or concerns came up during that process?

12. Would you be open to changing your course plan (in an event you don’t like what are studying)? 

13. If you could safely explore different study paths without affecting your plan, would you use it? How do you imagine you’d use it?

14. From a scale of 1 to 10, how hesitant or scared would you be to change your course path even with the guidance of the simulator?
