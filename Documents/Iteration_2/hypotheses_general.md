#  Hypotheses
1. We believe that matching students with a mentor who did the same dual-degree combo will increase confidence more than generic advice.
2. We believe that elective suggestions based on preferences (e.g., hates group work, avoids finals) will make choosing between similar options easier.
3. We believe that offering automatic alternatives when a course is full (summer option, swap, or path change) will keep students on track.
4. We believe that a “what if I change major/minor?” simulator will help students to explore options without fear of breaking their plan.
