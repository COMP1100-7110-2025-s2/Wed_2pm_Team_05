# Learning Cards

## Learning Card 1 : Students doing Dual Degrees will be more Confident they are assigned a Mentor. 
**Hypotheses**: We believe that matching students with a mentor who did the same dual-degree combo will increase confidence more than generic advice.  

**Observation**: Students prefer advice from mentors with dual-degree experience, as it feels more relevant and trustworthy than generic guidance.  

**Conclusion**: From that we learnt that students are more confident when they receive guidance from people who have successfully navigated the same dual-degree challenges, rather than from general advisors.  

**Action**: We will add a feature to our website for students to discuss their experiences with their degree and courses.  

---

## Learning Card 2 : Selecting electives would be easier if they are suggested based on their preferences.
**Hypotheses**: We believe that elective suggestions based on preferences (e.g., hates group work, avoids finals) will make choosing between similar options easier.  

**Observation**: Information like assignments and exams influence the selection of a course, and selecting a combination of courses for each semester.  

**Conclusion**: From that we learnt that students take assessment types into account when selecting electives, and tailoring course suggestions to match their preferences can make decision-making easier.  

**Action**: Our website will include information on assessment types for each course.  

---

## Learning Card 3 : Students will be More on Track if Automatic Alternatives were offered on the event they encounter a full course. 
**Hypotheses**: We believe that offering automatic alternatives when a course is full (summer option, swap, or path change) will keep students on track.  

**Observation**: Students doing dual degrees would be keen to explore options like a summer course as part of their degree.  

**Conclusion**: From that we learnt that students are more likely to remain on track if they are given clear alternatives when courses are unavailable.  

**Action**: We will provide students with information about alternatives such as summer courses or suitable course replacements to help them stay on track.  

---

## Learning Card 4 : Students will be able Explore Options without Fear if they have help from a Simulator Feature
**Hypotheses**: We believe that a “what if I change major/minor?” simulator will help students explore options without fear of breaking their plan.  

**Observation**: Students would use a simulator to get an outline of what their degree would look like if their major or minor changed, but would still prefer to speak to an academic advisor to confirm their decisions.  

**Conclusion**: From that we learnt that students value tools that let them experiment with degree changes, but they still need advisor confirmation to feel secure in their choices.  

**Action**: We will include an option to plan out a changed degree, and the ability to book an appointment with an academic advisor.
