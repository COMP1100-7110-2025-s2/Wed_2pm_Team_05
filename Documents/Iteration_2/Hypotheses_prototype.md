# Hypotheses for prototype
1. We believe that offering backup options (e.g., if a course is full, suggesting an equivalent or summer course) will keep students on track.
2. We believe that showing students a visual timeline of both degrees side by side will reduce confusion compared to just a list of courses.
3. We believe that short case studies from past dual-degree students (e.g., “How I managed my workload” or “Why I chose summer courses”) will help new students feel less overwhelmed.
4. We believe that providing a space to ask questions to past or current students (like a moderated forum or chat) will reduce reliance on trial-and-error planning.
