# Learning Cards

## Learning Card 1: Dual Degrees Have a Complicated Course Selection Process [test_cards.md](./test_cards.md#hypothesis-1-dual-degrees-have-a-complicated-course-selection-process-learning_cardsmd)

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believed that people doing dual degrees have a harder time picking courses than those who aren’t.

**OBSERVATION**
We observed that they struggled to choose their courses, as they often didn't have any electives, so there was no room for error. 

**CONCLUSION**
From that we learnt that most people later in their degrees (with more experience choosing courses) would change the course selection process to include more information, and add more details to course planners (like the semester that the course runs in).

**ACTION**
Therefore, we will put relevant details in a more accessible location, and filter out irrelevant courses based on major and minor. 


## Learning Card 2: Assessments in the Course Impact its Selection [test_cards.md](./test_cards.md#hypothesis-2-assessments-in-the-course-impact-its-selection-learning_cardsmd)

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believed that assessment types impact the selection of the course as they need to plan their course accordingly as many of them works and to maintain it they need to know about the assessment types.

**OBSERVATION**
We observed that it matters to people who get electives, but not to people who don't get much of a choice in their courses.

**CONCLUSION**
From that we learnt that the assessment in a course does not influence the course selection of people doing dual degrees beyond impacting when they take the course (courses with assessment that they don't like, they can do later in their degree).

**ACTION**
Therefore, we will place less import in including assessment details in the information relating to the course.


## Learning Card 3: Students Consider Feedback from Past Students [test_cards.md](./test_cards.md#hypothesis-3-students-consider-feedback-from-past-students-learning_cardsmd)

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believed that students care about feedback from past students and having access to it will make it easier.

**OBSERVATION**
We observed that students would take the opportunity to hear form past students/people who have previously taken the course.

**CONCLUSION**
From that we learnt that hearing from people first-hand helps student make decisions better than the course profile and using planners.

**ACTION**
Therefore, we will introduce a platform similar to Ed Discussion, where students can ask questions about courses.


## Learning Card 4: Students are Unhappy with the Course Selection Process [test_cards.md](./test_cards.md#hypothesis-4-students-are-unhappy-with-the-course-selection-process-learning_cardsmd)

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believed that students want an easier way to determine what courses they need to do and suitable for them.

**OBSERVATION**
We observed that it is a doable but long and tedious process, especially for first year students. It is easy to choose the wrong course and prolong your time at university.

**CONCLUSION**
From that we learnt that student would appreciate if the selection process was easier and the information was all accessible from the same location (currently, different pieces of information require different webpages).

**ACTION**
Therefore, we will include all relevant information in the same location, and filter this information based on degree.
