## Business Model Canvas

**Customer Segments**
- UQ students
- Students doing dual degrees

**Value Propositions**
- Discussion forum where students can ask other who have previously taken the course for advice
- Include more information about each course in an easily accessible location
- Filtered course display

**Channels**
- The UQ website

**Customer Relationships**
- Feedback feature in the website
- FAQ page
- Discussion board

**Revenue Streams**
- Long-term trust in the university due to the reliable service; therefore increase in enrollments

**Key Resoucres**
- UQ course list and requirements

**Key Activities**
- Make the website


### Accepted Hypotheses
People doing dual degrees have the hardest time picking courses.
Students care about feedback from past students and having access to it will make it easier.
Students want an easier way to determine what courses they need to do.

### Rejected Hypotheses
Assessment types impact the selection of the course.
