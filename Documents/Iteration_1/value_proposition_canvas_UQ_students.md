# Value Proposition Canvas - UQ Students

## Customer Profile

**The Jobs**
One of the main things that UQ students want to accomplish is to finish their degree on time. To achieve this, they need help with choosing the right courses and access to clear, reliable information about their degree structure. Students are in many different contexts depending on the degree they are studying, their year level, and their majors or interests. Their flexibility changes as they move through their studies, with some having room for electives while others must follow a strict structure. They will often interact with students in similar situations, as well as those who have already completed their degree, and look to these peers for guidance.

Most students have little time and are under considerable pressure to perform well. They want to avoid wasting time searching across different platforms or repeatedly checking whether they have selected the correct course. Accidentally taking the wrong subject can have major consequences for their graduation timeline. If there were a simple way to input their details and immediately see exactly what courses they need to take, it would relieve a huge amount of pressure and give them confidence that they are on the right track.

**Gains**
Students would greatly value the time saved by having this process streamlined. They want a system that is easy to use, intuitive, and accurate, one that doesn’t break or lead to further confusion. They expect high performance and quality and are motivated by tools that make their lives easier, flatten the learning curve, and provide reliable information at lower effort.

Socially, students want to be seen as capable, organised, and in control of their studies. They want to feel secure and confident in the choices they make and know that they are not falling behind. Their aspirations are to graduate smoothly, reduce unnecessary stress, and have the reassurance that they are doing the right thing. Success for them is measured by staying on track with their degree, minimising errors, and managing their time effectively.

**Pains of Customers**
Many students spend far too much time trying to figure out how their courses fit together. This can leave them stressed, frustrated, and uncertain about whether they are making the right decisions. Current systems often underperform because information is scattered across different sources, sometimes inconsistent, and difficult to interpret. Students face challenges in understanding how their degree works, working out subject prerequisites, and knowing what to take and when.

These challenges carry emotional and social consequences. Students may fear appearing disorganised or less capable than their peers. They worry about making mistakes that could cost them financially or delay their graduation, and this can weigh heavily on their wellbeing. Common issues include enrolling in the wrong subject, misinterpreting degree requirements, or failing to meet prerequisites in time. Barriers to adopting new tools include concerns about accuracy, steep learning curves, or doubts about whether it will actually make their lives easier.

## Value Map

**Pain Relievers**
Our product will reduce the time students spend planning their courses by automating much of the process. It will relieve stress by providing clear and accurate guidance, reduce the risk of mistakes that can delay graduation, and eliminate the frustration that comes from using unreliable or confusing systems. By consolidating all the relevant information into one place, it ensures that students can trust the outcomes without needing to constantly second-guess themselves.
