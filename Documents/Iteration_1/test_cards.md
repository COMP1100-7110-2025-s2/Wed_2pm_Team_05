# Test Cards

## Hypothesis 1: Dual Degrees Have a Complicated Course Selection Process [learning_cards.md](./learning_cards#learning-card-1-dual-degrees-have-a-complicated-course-selection-process-test_cardsmd)

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that people doing dual degrees have a harder time picking courses than those who aren’t.

**TEST**
To verify that, we will interview people doing dual degrees, and people not doing dual degrees.

**METRIC**
And measure their level of dissatisfaction with the course selection process.

**CRITERIA**
We are right if people doing dual degrees report that they have a difficult time choosing their courses, and people not doing dual degrees have little to no issues with the course selection process.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1. What process did you go through to pick your courses for this semester?
2. Have you ever done a course that you thought was necessary for your degree, but wasn’t? If so, what caused this to happen?

## Hypothesis 3: Students Consider Feedback from Past Students [learning_cards.md](./learning_cards#learning-card-3-students-consider-feedback-from-past-students-test_cardsmd)

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that students care about feedback from past students and having access to it will make it easier.

**TEST**
To verify that, we will conduct interviews asking about decision making process for courses selection. 

**METRIC**
And measure how often people mentioning reviews (eg reddit, friends, senior).

**CRITERIA**
We are right if students consistently say that reviews make course selection easier or significantly influence their course choices.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1. Did you talk to a past student or academic advisor when deciding what courses to pick?
2. If you had the option to talk to a past student about choosing courses, would you?


## Hypothesis 4: Students are Unhappy with the Course Selection Process [learning_cards.md](./learning_cards#learning-card-4-students-are-unhappy-with-the-course-selection-process-test_cardsmd)

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that students want an easier way to determine what courses they need to do.

**TEST**
To verify that, we will interview students.

**METRIC**
And measure their dissatisfaction with the course selection process. 

**CRITERIA**
We are right if they appear to be dissatisfied with the current ease of course selection, and suggest improvements.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1. If you could improve the course selection process, would you? How?
2. Do you think there should be more information available about the course? If so, what information?
3. Have you ever done a course that you thought was necessary for your degree, but wasn’t? If so, what caused this to happen?
