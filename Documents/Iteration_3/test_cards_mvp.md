# Test Cards For MVP

## Hypothesis 1: Students will feel more personally connected and supported if they are offered backup mentor matching based on shared interests.

**HYPOTHESIS** 
We believe that offering mentor-matching based on shared interests (not just degree combination) will make students feel more personally connected and supported.

**TEST** 
To verify that, we will conduct interviews with students and ask them about their interests and concerns on being matched with a mentor.

**METRIC** 
And measure the level of ease and support the students feel based on being matched with a mentor.

**CRITERIA**
We are right if most of the students report that they will feel much supported and personally connected if they have access to mentor matching based on shared interests.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1.	What kind of shared interests would make you feel most connected to a mentor?
2.	What concerns, if any, might you have about matching mentors this way?
3.	How supportive will it be for you if you are matched with a mentor based on shared interests?

## Hypothesis 2: Incorporating peer ratings or student reviews side by side would be much reliable to students compared to a system-generated recommendations.

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that incorporating peer ratings or student reviews for electives will increase trust in course suggestions compared to system-generated recommendations alone.

**TEST**
To verify that, we will interview students and ask them whether reading student reviews for electives will be more reliable compared to system recommendations.

**METRIC**
And measure how much more reliable it will be for them if they were shown a course planner including student reviews and feedback for electives.

**CRITERIA**
We are right if most students report that they would think it will be more reliable if they were to see student reviews included when using our product instead of just system suggestions.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1.	How would you feel if both system suggestions and student reviews were presented together — which would you rely on more?
2.	If you could see reviews or feedback from other students directly on the planner, what type of information would you want included?
3.	On a scale from 1–10, how much easier would you feel if you have student reviews added to your course planner?

## Hypothesis 3: Students will have a better sense of control over their academic timeline if they have access to the option of adding alternatives.

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that integrating “add alternative” planning options in the degree planner will improve students’ sense of control over their academic timeline.

**TEST**
To verify that, we will conduct interviews with students and ask them whether integrating “add alternative” planning options will give them a better sense of control.

**METRIC**
And measure how much more confident they think they will be with academic journey with the “add alternative” planning options.

**CRITERIA**
We are right if most students say they will feel a better sense of control and confidence with the access to the option of adding alternatives.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1.	On a scale from 1–10, how much control would you feel you have with this “add alternative” option compared to your current process?
2.	How easy or difficult is it currently to understand what would change if you switched your major, minor, or electives?
3.	How much more confident would you be of your academic journey if you have this access to this planning option?

## Hypothesis 4: Students will be able to make more informed and confident decisions without fear if they have a visual showing their progress comparisons.

**HYPOTHESIS**
We believe that showing visual progress comparisons (current path vs new path) will help students make more informed and confident decisions.

**TEST**
To verify that, we will interview students accordingly and ask them if having a visual progress comparison for their current and new path will help them be more decisive and confident.

**METRIC**
And measure how much more confident and decisive on planning their courses if shown a visual progress of comparisons.

**CRITERIA**
We are right if more students feel they would make more informed decisions with access to a visual timeline of their progress comparisons.

**INTERVIEW QUESTIONS**
1.	What kind of features or visuals would make managing your dual degree feel easier?
2.	Would seeing this visual comparison make you feel more confident about making academic changes? Why or why not?
3.	Would this feature reduce your need to ask an advisor for confirmation, or would you still want advisor input?
