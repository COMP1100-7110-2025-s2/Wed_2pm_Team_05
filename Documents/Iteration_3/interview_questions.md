# Interview Questions for Iteration 3
1. What kind of shared interests would make you feel most connected to a mentor?
2. If our website automatically mapped both degree requirements together, how helpful would that be (scale 1–10)?
3. What concerns, if any, might you have about matching mentors this way?
4. What kind of features or visuals would make managing your dual degree feel easier?
5. How would you feel if both system suggestions and student reviews were presented together — which would you rely on more?
6. If you could see reviews or feedback from other students directly on the planner, what type of information would you want included?
7. On a scale from 1–10, how much control would you feel you have with this “add alternative” option compared to your current process?
8. How easy or difficult is it currently to understand what would change if you switched your major, minor, or electives?
9. Would seeing this visual comparison make you feel more confident about making academic changes? Why or why not?
10. Would this feature reduce your need to ask an advisor for confirmation, or would you still want advisor input?
11. What kind of backup options would you like to see when a course isn’t available?
