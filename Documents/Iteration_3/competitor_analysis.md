# Competitor Analysis

## Unique Value Proposition (UVP)
Our project, **UQ Courses**, is designed to solve the problem of fragmented and unintuitive course planning for UQ students. Right now, students have to use multiple different websites and platforms if they want to find course information, check program rules, plan their degree, and hear about other students’ experiences. This process is time-consuming and frustrating, and often students end up turning to Reddit or friends because the official systems are too confusing.  

We believe our unique value proposition is to provide a centralise platform for UQ students that integrates official course/program data with interactive degree planning tools and peer reviews. Unlike current solutions, our platform would be both easy to use, accurate and student-friendly

## SWOT Analysis of Key Competitors

### 1. Newcastle University Degree Planner
- **Strengths**: Reliable and validated by the university’s data systems. Provides accurate rule checking and progression tracking.  
- **Weaknesses**: Static interface, limited interactivity, and no peer or community features.  
- **Opportunities**: Expand interactivity and visual planning inspired by Newcastle’s accuracy model.  
- **Threats**: Universities may adopt similar all-in-one planners.  

---

### 2. Notion Planner Templates
- **Strengths**: Extremely flexible and easy to personalise for different degrees or goals.  
- **Weaknesses**: Manual input required; no official data connection or automation.  
- **Opportunities**: UQ Courses can automate what Notion users currently build manually.  
- **Threats**: Some students may prefer custom tools they can fully control.  

---

### 3. UQ Planner (uqplanner.app)
- **Strengths**: Visual and student-friendly interface with drag-and-drop planning.  
- **Weaknesses**: Limited to static data, lacks real-time updates and peer review integration.  
- **Opportunities**: Expand on this idea with smarter data validation, student feedback, and personalisation.  
- **Threats**: UQ could release a similar internal version that competes directly.  

---

### 4. Griffith Course Profiles
- **Strengths**: Official and detailed source of course information, updated regularly.  
- **Weaknesses**: Text-heavy, not interactive, and lacks visual appeal or review functionality.  
- **Opportunities**: Improve usability and visualisation for course profiles at UQ.  
- **Threats**: If UQ adopts a similar structure, overlap could increase.  

---

### 5. Quora
- **Strengths**: Provides open, candid peer perspectives and global community discussion.  
- **Weaknesses**: Unstructured, lacks accuracy and institutional verification.  
- **Opportunities**: UQ Courses can capture the same peer insight in a structured, trusted format.  
- **Threats**: Students may still use Quora for broader, informal discussions.  

---

### 6. 💡 UQ Courses (MVP)
- **Strengths**: The only platform combining verified UQ data, peer reviews,  course display, and interactive planning.  
- **Weaknesses**: limited data and reach, not integrated with UQ official
- **Opportunities**: Expand integration with UQ APIs, add social and mentoring features, and scale across faculties.  
- **Threats**: Potential overlap if UQ develops a similar integrated platform.  

---

## Insights and Strategy

**Insights**
- Most official planners (UQ, Newcastle, Griffith) focus on accuracy and compliance but lack usability and peer engagement  
- Community-based tools (Quora, Notion) offer student perspective and flexibility, but lack verified academic structure.  
- Students want an experience that feels **personalised, interactive, and trustworthy**, not just informational.  

**Strategy**
1. **Integration**: Combine the reliability of official data sources with user-generated insights and structured reviews.  
2. **Visualisation**: Deliver interactive GenZ Design
3. **Personalisation**: Recommend electives and alternatives based on student preferences and availability.  
4. **Community + Trust**: Provide a credible platform where student voices are backed by real data.
