## Interview 4 - Kiel

**Q: What degree are you doing?**  
A: Psychology  

**Q: What year of your degree are you in?**  
A: Final year.  

**Q: What process did you go through to pick your courses for this semester?**  
A: I had mostly electives left, so I chose lighter ones to balance with my thesis project.  

**Q: Have you ever done a course that you thought was necessary for your degree, but wasn’t?**  
A: Yeah, I once took a course thinking it was compulsory.  

**Q: If so, what caused this to happen?**  
A: Miscommunication from an advisor.  

**Q: Did you plan out your courses for your whole degree, or semester-by-semester?**  
A: I planned most of it early on to avoid clashes later.  

**Q: Does the assessment on the course profile play a part in your decision to choose the course? Why?**  
A: Yeah, I prefer courses without too many group projects. They’re just harder to manage.  

**Q: Do you think there should be more information available about the course? If so, what information?**  
A: More clarity on weekly workload would be nice.  

**Q: Did you talk to a past student or academic advisor when deciding what courses to pick?**  
A: Yeah, I asked seniors a lot in my second year.  

**Q: If you had the option to talk to a past student about choosing courses, would you?**  
A: Of course, I already do that.  

**Q: If you could improve the course selection process, would you? How?**  
A: I’d allow students to “trial” a course for a week before locking it in.
