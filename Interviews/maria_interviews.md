## Interview 2 - Raisa

Maria: Was it clear what courses were required for your course?

Maria: What factors are most important when you choose courses? (eg timetable, prerequisites)

Maria: What degree are you taking and what courses are you taking currently?
Raisa: I’m in Computer Science, and right now I’m taking Statistical Modeling and Analysis, Microeconomics, Relational Database, Fundamental to Data Science

Maria: Have you regretted taking a certain course?

Maria: Which courses do you suggest and not suggest taking together?

Maria: Is there a course you wouldn’t take if it was clear certain classes like studios and applied classes are required for their selected courses?
Raisa: Some courses I wouldn’t take due to having practical class

Maria: Do you visit the course profile on the website prior to enrollment?
Raisa: Yes, always that’s usually where I check the assessment breakdown before deciding
