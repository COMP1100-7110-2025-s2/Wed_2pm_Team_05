## Interview 1 - Omar

**Q: What degree are you doing?**  
A: I am doing Computer Science  

**Q: What year of your degree are you in?**  
A: Second year  

**Q: What process did you go through to pick your courses for this semester?**  
A: Checked the study plan, then the course profile on the UQ website also I always asked friends what they were taking. Also made sure I didn’t have 8am lectures  

**Q: Have you ever done a course that you thought was necessary for your degree, but wasn’t?**  
A: Yeah, a stats elective. Thought it was required but it wasn’t, it was tough i kinda regret taking it  

**Q: If so, what caused this to happen?**  
A: I misread the program plan. It looked like a core course but I was wrong  

**Q: Did you plan out your courses for your whole degree, or semester-by-semester?**  
A: Semester-by-semester. I don’t like locking in too far ahead.  

**Q: Does the assessment on the course profile play a part in your decision to choose the course? Why?**  
A: Yeah. If it’s too exam heavy, I’ll avoid it.  

**Q: Do you think there should be more information available about the course? If so, what information?**  
A: More details on actual workload and especially the difficulty of the course, I want to know if I can balance the load within other compulsory courses  

**Q: Did you talk to a past student or academic advisor when deciding what courses to pick?**  
A: I asked a senior who’d done the course and I look up some review on reddit  

**Q: If you had the option to talk to a past student about choosing courses, would you?**  
A: Definitely, that’s the most useful advice and I trust them more than then course profile  

**Q: If you could improve the course selection process, would you? How?**  
A: I’d make the planner more interactive with clear clash warnings.
