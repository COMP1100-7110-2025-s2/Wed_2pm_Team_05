## Interview 2 - Raisa

**Q: When choosing courses, what do you have most trouble with?**  
A: I mostly find it difficult to plan ahead, like deciding which semester is the best time to take certain courses so I don’t overload myself  

**Q: Was it clear what courses were required for your course?**  
A: Sometimes it’s clear, but for electives I usually need to double-check the handbook or ask seniors to be sure  

**Q: What factors are most important when you choose courses? (eg timetable, prerequisites)**  
A: I pay attention to prerequisites and the type of assessment. I prefer courses with more projects or assignments instead of exams  

**Q: What degree are you taking and what courses are you taking currently?**  
A: I’m in Computer Science, and right now I’m taking Statistical Modeling and Analysis, Microeconomics, Relational Database, Fundamental to Data Science  

**Q: Have you regretted taking a certain course?**  
A: Yes, once I took a course that was very theory-heavy, and I realized I enjoy more practical or applied classes  

**Q: Which courses do you suggest and not suggest taking together?**  
A: I’d avoid taking two math-heavy courses in the same semester. It becomes too much calculation and theory at once  

**Q: Have you missed enrolling in a course because it was full?**  
A: Not yet, but I usually register early because I’ve heard it happens often  

**Q: Which courses do you like or not like?**  
A: I like courses where the lecturer is engaging and gives real-world examples. I don’t like courses that only rely on lectures and very little interaction  

**Q: Is there a course you wouldn’t take if it was clear certain classes like studios and applied classes are required for their selected courses?**  
A: Some courses I wouldn’t take due to having practical class  

**Q: Do you visit the course profile on the website prior to enrollment?**  
A: Yes, always that’s usually where I check the assessment breakdown before deciding
